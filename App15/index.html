Memorise these before playing!
👁 1. VISION
Scope: Nation/System Level · Very Long-term · Not Measurable
The highest, broadest educational dream or philosophy. Inspirational, value-based, and philosophical. Sets the overarching direction for an entire education system.
Key Question: "What kind of society do we want to create?"
🎯 2. AIM
Scope: Education Level (General) · Long-term · Difficult to Measure
Broad educational purposes derived from Vision. They describe WHY we are educating learners. Developmental and purpose-oriented.
Key Question: "Why are we educating learners?"
📖 3. CURRICULAR GOALS (CGs)
Scope: Stage/Subject Level · Medium-term · Partly Measurable
Broad learning expectations for a stage or subject. Translates aims into curriculum directions. More specific than aims, stage-wise and curriculum-oriented.
Key Question: "What broad learning should happen at this stage/subject?"
🧠 4. COMPETENCIES
Scope: Subject/Grade Level · Short-term · Measurable
Integrated abilities learners must demonstrate — combining knowledge, skills, values, attitudes and application. Action-oriented, observable and performance-based.
Key Question: "What should learners be able to DO with what they learn?"
✅ 5. LEARNING OUTCOMES (LOs)
Scope: Lesson/Unit Level · Immediate · Easily Measurable
Specific and measurable achievements expected after a lesson/unit/activity. Shows what learners actually achieve. Very specific, observable and assessable.
Key Question: "What exactly will the learner achieve after THIS lesson/unit?"
