Biology Handbook

Cell Organelles

Nucleus — control centre, holds DNA.
Mitochondria — powerhouse, releases energy via respiration.
Chloroplast — site of photosynthesis (plants only).
Golgi body — packages & ships proteins.
Ribosomes — protein synthesis.
Vacuole — storage of water, ions, waste.

Human Organ Systems

Digestive, Respiratory, Circulatory, Nervous, Excretory, Endocrine, Skeletal, Muscular — each maintains homeostasis and works with the others.

Plant Tissues

Xylem — transports water & minerals upward (dead cells).
Phloem — transports food, mainly sugars (living cells).
Meristem — actively dividing growth tissue.

Genetics Basics

Gene — unit of heredity. Allele — version of a gene. Dominant allele masks recessive in heterozygotes. Genotype = genetic makeup; Phenotype = observable trait.

Classification Levels

Kingdom → Phylum → Class → Order → Family → Genus → Species.

Measurement Units

Cell size: micrometres (µm). Heart rate: beats per minute (bpm). Blood pressure: mmHg. Body temperature: °C.

Vision

To transform biology education from passive memorisation into active, inquiry-based exploration — giving every student, regardless of resources, access to a fully-equipped virtual laboratory.

Why Virtual Laboratories

Virtual labs let students repeat experiments safely, manipulate variables freely, and visualise microscopic and internal processes that a physical classroom lab cannot show.

Pedagogy

Built on constructivist and competency-based principles: students form hypotheses, test them, observe outcomes, and construct understanding through guided discovery — directly aligned with NEP 2020.

Environmental & Scientific Literacy

Modules on ecosystems and plant biology build environmental awareness alongside core scientific literacy and lab skills.
