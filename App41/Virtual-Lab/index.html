Vision

Virtual Economics Laboratory was created to move economics teaching away from rote definitions and towards inquiry: students should be able to move a slider, break a market, rebuild a budget, and feel the trade-offs that economic actors face every day. The lab is built entirely offline so it works reliably on shared classroom devices, smart boards and low-end Android phones across Kashmir's schools — no data connection, login or cloud service required.

  • Experiential, inquiry-based learning over memorisation
  • Financial literacy and responsible economic citizenship for every student
  • Full alignment with NEP 2020 and Competency-Based Learning
  • Entrepreneurship education and evidence-based decision-making
  • Accessible design for CWSN learners and low-end devices
  • A single consistent design language across the whole Virtual Simulations ecosystem
