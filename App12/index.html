How to Play

🕵️ Your Mission

You are a Word Detective! Analyze each sentence and identify all the nouns, verbs, and adjectives hidden inside. Tap a word category below, then tap the words in the sentence.

Color Code

  • 🟦 NOUN — Person, place, or thing
  • 🟥 VERB — Action or state
  • 🟩 ADJECTIVE — Describes a noun

🏆 Scoring

✅ Correct Noun+1 pt
✅ Correct Verb+2 pts
✅ Correct Adjective+2 pts
🎯 Full Sentence Correct+5 bonus!
❌ Wrong Answer-1 pt

⏱️ Levels

  • Level 1 Easy — Simple sentences, 60s timer
  • Level 2 Medium — Mixed vocabulary, 50s timer
  • Level 3 Hard — Complex sentences, 40s timer
  • Level 4 Expert — Tricky words, 30s timer
  • 🔥 Bonus — Ultimate challenge!
