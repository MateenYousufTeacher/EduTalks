🎯 Vision

The Virtual Political Science Laboratory was created to help students experience democracy and governance rather than simply read about them — building constitutional literacy, critical thinking and responsible citizenship through hands-on, inquiry-based simulation.

📐 Design Principles

  • Inquiry-based, competency-based learning aligned with NEP 2020
  • Every module is a genuine simulation — not a static slideshow
  • 100% offline, installable, and accessible on low-end devices
  • Constitutionally accurate, politically neutral, curriculum-aligned content
